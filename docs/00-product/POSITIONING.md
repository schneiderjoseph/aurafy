# AURAFY — Positioning

## One liner

**AURAFY** is where people discover their next look and book it — and where studios run the business that delivers it.

## Category

```text
AURAFY
Beauty discovery · Social booking · Studio operating system
```

Not:

```text
"Salon management software"
```

## Tagline

> **Discover your look. Book your moment.**

## Who it is for

| Actor | Job |
|---|---|
| **Consumer** | Find looks, follow studios, chat, book, rebook |
| **Studio owner** | Calendar, staff, CRM, inventory, analytics, billing |
| **Staff** | Today’s agenda, complete jobs, photos, tips |
| **Platform admin** | Tenants, plans, abuse, health |

Verticals (same core): nails, barber, hair, makeup, lashes/brows, spa, wellness — later tattoo/piercing if policies allow.

## What it is not

- A single-studio internal tool dressed as a platform
- Instagram without booking
- Treatwell/Fresha clone without a social graph
- A subdomain-per-tenant consumer product (profiles are path-based: `/@user`, `/studio/:slug`)
- An ERP / full accounting suite
- A place that stores private customer photos as public CDN blobs

## Brand

| Item | Value |
|---|---|

| Version | **0.0.0** (spec only) |
| Primary domain (intent) | `aurafy.app` |
| `.com` | Parked / for sale (~$34k) — **do not buy at this stage** |

Trademark / social handle clearance is **to-verify** before public launch — do not treat casual availability as legal clearance.

## Growth loop

```text
Free studios publish looks
        ↓
Feed acquires consumers
        ↓
Bookings create studio value
        ↓
Studios upgrade (Pro / Business)
        ↓
More studios → more content
```

Free must be **usable** for a real studio (calendar, customers, booking). Paid sells power, automation, and growth — not a crippled demo.
