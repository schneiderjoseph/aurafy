# Brand — AURAFY

**Version:** 0.0.0 · **Status:** DECIDED (initial)  
**Tagline:** Discover your next look.

---

## Positioning

AURAFY is **not** a “salon pink” beauty brand and **not** an accounting SaaS look.

```text
Consumer:  the place where people discover their next look
Studio:    where craft becomes appointments
```

Concept: **aura** = identity / style put into the world — not mysticism.

---

## Visual direction

**Premium · Editorial · Social · Modern · Warm**

Spirit: Pinterest × Instagram × Linear × Apple — with an AURAFY identity.

Shell: **neutral luxury**. Imagery provides most of the color. Brand violet is an **accent**, not a purple wallpaper.

---

Avoid: classic beauty pink as primary · glassmorphism everywhere · heavy multi-layer shadows · purple gradients filling the viewport.

## Typography

| Role | Font |
|---|---|
| UI / brand wordmark | **Manrope** (or Plus Jakarta Sans if Manrope unavailable) |
| Editorial accent | **Instrument Serif** (or DM Serif Display) |

Example:

```text
AURAFY                          ← sans, clean
Discover your next look.        ← serif accent OK on marketing
```

---

## Logo

- **Wordmark** first: `AURAFY`
- Distinctive detail on **A** or **Y** (subtle aura / spark) — optional
- **No** scissors, polish, lips, faces, diamonds, flowers
- Must work in **black and white**
- App icon: monogram **A** + spark/orbit mark
- Optional signature glyph: ✦ — sparingly

---

## Tone of voice

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| Manage your beauty business efficiently | Discover your next look. |
| Synergy / leverage / solution | See it. Save it. Book it. |
| | Show your work. Grow your studio. |
| | Create. Get discovered. Get booked. |

Primary slogan: **Discover your next look.**

---

## Two modes

### Consumer

Large photography, immersive feed, bottom sheets, expressive motion, rounded media.

### Studio

Clear sidebar, calendar, tables, filters, financial summaries — breathe; no 17-widget dashboards.
